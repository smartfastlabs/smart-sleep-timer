## Description (4000)

Smart Sleep Timer puts your Mac to sleep when you mean to stop.

Start a timer before a show, a podcast, or an audiobook, and your Mac goes to sleep when it ends instead of running until morning. Set a bedtime and it happens every night without a timer at all. Turn on Lights Out and your Mac keeps going back to sleep until you wake up for real.

It lives in your menu bar, makes no network connections, and does exactly one thing.

QUICK TIMERS
Click the moon, pick 15 minutes to 2 hours, done. The popover shows exactly when your Mac will sleep, and the icon changes shape as it gets close.

IT ASKS FIRST
If you've touched the keyboard or mouse recently when a timer ends, a full-screen countdown gives you ten seconds and four choices: 15 more minutes, 30 more minutes, 1 more hour, or not tonight. If you've stepped away, there's no countdown. Your Mac sleeps silently.

BEDTIME
Set a bedtime and a wake time. At bedtime your Mac goes to sleep, or shows the countdown if you're mid-sentence. Bedtime lasts until wake time, so 10 PM to 6 AM is one night, and midnight doesn't confuse it.

LIGHTS OUT
For everyone who wakes the Mac at 1 AM "just to check something." Between bedtime and wake time, Lights Out puts the Mac back to sleep a set number of minutes after every wake. Snoozing buys another round. "Not tonight" turns it off until morning.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No analytics. No network access. It reads how long you've been idle from the system, which needs no special permission, and runs inside the macOS App Sandbox.

SAVE POWER
A Mac that sleeps when you do uses less energy and, on a laptop, spends fewer cycles on the battery.

Requires macOS 15 or later.

## What's New in This Version (4000)

Version 1.1 is a ground-up update.

• Countdown before sleep: if you're still using your Mac when a timer ends, a full-screen countdown appears with 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, or Not tonight. Idle Macs still sleep silently.
• Bedtime is now a window: set a bedtime and a wake time, and it works correctly across midnight.
• Lights Out mode: after bedtime, your Mac goes back to sleep a set number of minutes after every wake, until morning.
• Choose how long you must be idle before the Mac sleeps without asking.
• New icon, new menu bar glyph that shows what's coming, and a redesigned popover and Settings window.
• Faster, lighter, and rebuilt for macOS 15 and later.
